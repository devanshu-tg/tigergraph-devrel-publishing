The Agentic GraphRAG Hackathon by TigerGraph explores when an AI agent actually earns its cost. Some questions can be answered with a single retrieval step. Harder ones need a system that breaks the question apart, decides what to retrieve, checks the evidence it gets back, and keeps investigating until it has enough to answer.

Teams built three pipelines on TigerGraph over a corpus of about 2,950 Wikipedia articles on Olympic events:

1. **RAG**: retrieval from a vector index.
2. **GraphRAG**: a fixed retrieval pipeline over a knowledge graph.
3. **Agentic GraphRAG**: an agent that plans, chooses its tools (entity linking, graph traversal, aggregation, temporal hops, similarity search) and decides when to stop.

Each pipeline was benchmarked on 100 public questions and 50 hidden questions covering lookups, multi-hop, temporal, aggregation and superlative questions, comparing accuracy, tokens per question and latency.

## Rounds

- **Round 1**: **57 teams** submitted. Submissions closed in early October 2026. The top 25 teams were invited to a 10-minute judging session.
- **Round 2**: the top 15 teams from those sessions move on.
