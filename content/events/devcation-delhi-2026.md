Devcation Delhi 2026 was a student hackathon hosted by GDG on Campus IGDTUW in collaboration with GDG IIT Delhi, open to students from all colleges. It opened with a ceremony at the IGDTUW Auditorium on 25 March 2026.

TigerGraph ran a track at Devcation that challenged teams to build their solution on TigerGraph: model the problem as a graph, query it with GSQL, and use multi-hop relationships to power features that a regular database would struggle with.

## What teams submitted

**91 teams** submitted projects to the TigerGraph track. Each team submitted a GitHub repository, a slide deck, a demo video, a deployed link where available, and a short write-up of how they integrated TigerGraph into their solution. Projects covered fraud detection, healthcare, cybersecurity, smart cities, agriculture, logistics and more.

## Winners

- **Graphites**: NetraVote
- **GridSentinals**: Resilience.AI
