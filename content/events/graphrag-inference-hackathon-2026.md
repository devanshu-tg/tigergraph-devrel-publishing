The GraphRAG Inference Hackathon by TigerGraph asked one question: can a knowledge graph make LLM inference faster, cheaper and smarter than plain retrieval?

Teams built three pipelines side by side, on the same data and with the same model:

1. **LLM only**: the question goes straight to the model, with no retrieval.
2. **Basic RAG**: text chunks retrieved from a vector database.
3. **GraphRAG**: context retrieved by traversing a TigerGraph knowledge graph.

The headline metric was token reduction with maintained accuracy. Every team reported tokens, cost and latency per query for each pipeline, and measured answer quality with an LLM-as-a-judge pass rate and BERTScore F1.

## Rounds

In total, **107 projects** were submitted across the three rounds.


- **Round 1** (97 teams): an open benchmark on a dataset of each team's choice, with a live comparison dashboard, a demo video and a write-up.
- **Round 2** (9 finalists): finalists scaled their systems to much larger corpora and improved their pipelines based on the judges' feedback.
- **Round 3** (8 finalists): the Generalizable Context Optimization Challenge, where finalists answered a common set of questions over SEC filings from S&P 100 companies.

Participants got free access to TigerGraph Savanna, TigerGraph Community Edition, the TigerGraph MCP server and the TigerGraph GraphRAG repository, plus mentoring from the TigerGraph team. The hackathon ran on Unstop, with Builder Base as community partner.

## Winners

**Round 2**

- 1st place: **Riseon**, GraphRAG on U.S. case law
- 2nd place: **MasterGraph**, token-efficient LLM inference on a biomedical dataset
- 3rd place: **Masterminds**, GraphRAG Inference Engine

**Round 3**

- 1st place: **MasterGraph**, generalizable context optimization on SEC filings
- 2nd place: **Aayush & Anushka**, The Token Ledger
