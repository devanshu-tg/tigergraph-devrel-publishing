## Notes from the team

Nothing major; the challenge was well organised and TigerGraph was great to build on. Our only constraint was time: we had to leave out parts of our original plan, like more accurate grouping of related transactions and a live monitoring mode that scans for new alerts on its own. Thank you for the opportunity; we'd love to keep building on it.

## Details

- **LLM:** Google Gemini (gemini-3.7-flash and gemini-3.5-flash-lite)
- **Agent framework:** Custom Python agent (no third-party framework such as LangChain or CrewAI), with TigerGraph MCP for tool access.
- **TigerGraph deployment:** Savanna
