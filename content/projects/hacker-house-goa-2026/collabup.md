## Notes from the team

Thank you for a benchmark with real traps in it — the false-positive problem forced us to build something more honest than a classifier, and we learned a lot in the process. We're also glad the conversation is continuing beyond the hackathon: we have a discovery call scheduled with Sebastien Mancel and the team, and we're looking forward to exploring where TigerGraph fits into what we're building next at CollabUp and onsumm.ai. Sentinel proved to us that graph-native facts plus a calibrated decision layer is a pattern we want to carry into our products, so we'd welcome a deeper collaboration — whether that's on the graph side, the agentic tooling, or going to market together.
