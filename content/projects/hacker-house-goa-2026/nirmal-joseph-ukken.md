## Notes from the team

I discovered Hacker House Goa relatively late, after the first three tasks had already finished, and joined specifically for Task 4. I originally thought “why not?” after seeing the opportunity to build something interesting and potentially make it to Goa. The challenge ended up being a great excuse to dive deeply into TigerGraph, MCP, GraphRAG and agentic investigation workflows.

## Details

- **LLM:** Google Gemini (gemini-3.5-flash-lite)
- **Agent framework:** Custom Python state-machine agent using the official TigerGraph MCP server (tigergraph-mcp) as its tool layer, Gemini function calling, a policy engine as code, and a Streamlit UI.
- **TigerGraph deployment:** Savanna
