## Notes from the team

One thing I really appreciated was how naturally TigerGraph fit the fraud-investigation use case. The ability to connect transactions, customers, cards, devices, and historical cases made patterns visible that would be difficult to capture with a traditional relational approach.

The combination of TigerGraph, MCP, and GraphRAG also made it possible to build a more explainable agent rather than treating the LLM as the decision-maker. I’d be excited to see TigerGraph continue investing in developer tooling, MCP/agent integrations, and simpler onboarding for AI/GraphRAG applications.

## Details

- **LLM:** qwen/qwen3.8-27b from groq API
- **Agent framework:** LangGraph.js
- **TigerGraph deployment:** Savanna
