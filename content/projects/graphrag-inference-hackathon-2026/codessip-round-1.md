ECEGraphAI is a GraphRAG-based educational assistant designed for Electronics and
Communication Engineering (ECE). The system compares three AI pipelines side-by-side:
LLM-only, Basic RAG, and GraphRAG. It uses structured graph relationships to retrieve highly
relevant information instead of sending large document chunks directly to the LLM. The goal is
to reduce token consumption, lower inference cost, improve response speed, and maintain
answer accuracy. The project includes an interactive comparison dashboard that displays
responses and performance metrics across all three pipelines.

## Dataset

Domain: Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE), Educational Content, Technical QA, Research Literature , Datasets used: MIT OpenCourseWare Electrical Engineering notes and assignments NPTEL ECE course transcripts and PDFs All About Circuits educational content OpenStax Engineering/Electronics textbooks HotpotQA SQuAD Natural Questions arXiv ECE papers: Signal Processing Analog Electronics Embedded Systems Communication Systems VLSI

## Benchmark results

Token reduction (GraphRAG vs Basic RAG): 47.3%
Cost reduction: 48.8%
Latency improvement: 33.3%
Accuracy maintained and improved using graph-based multi-hop retrieval
