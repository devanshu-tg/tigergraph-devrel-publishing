GraphMind is a GraphRAG-based inference comparison platform that evaluates three AI retrieval architectures: raw LLM inference, traditional vector RAG, and knowledge graph-powered GraphRAG. The system uses interconnected AI research papers to demonstrate how graph-aware retrieval improves multi-hop reasoning, reduces token usage, lowers inference latency, and delivers more contextually relevant responses compared to standard RAG approaches

## Dataset

Domain: Biomedical Research (Neurology & Neurodegenerative Diseases) — specifically targeting Alzheimer's Disease (AD), Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), ADHD, Dyscalculia, and Dyslexia. Source: PubMed Central (PMC) Open Access Repository (National Institutes of Health / National Library of Medicine).

## Benchmark results

Token Reduction % = 80% in Graph RAG compared to Basic RAG
Cost per Query = 0.0090 - Baseline LLM , 0.018 - basic RAG , 0.0036 - GraphRAG
Latency - 5062 - Baseline LLM , 2592ms - basic RAG , 1610ms - GraphRAG
Accuracy - Fail + 64 - Baseline LLM, Pass + 82 Basic RAG  , Pass + 95 -GraphRAG
