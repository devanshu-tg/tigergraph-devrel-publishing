PAPERSLASH , a project based on making research simpler .It is able to not just find the documents but find connections which can help users in their research. It can provide a better recommendation to future readings, at lower tokens and at lesser latency. Our team made use of savanna as well there were mutliple agents as well which used some inbuilt algorithms like pageranker and centrality to further improve the bertscore and llm-as-judge results.

## Dataset

ACADEMIA AND RESEARCH , https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/johoetter/design-thinking-arxiv

## Benchmark results

Token Supremacy: GraphRAG achieved a 40% token reduction over traditional RAG and a massive 75% reduction over the LLM baseline by eliminating noise and targeting relationships.
Cost Efficiency: At $0.00004 per query, GraphRAG is the most sustainable architecture for large-scale research analysis.
Lightning Speed: GraphRAG is 97% faster than our vector-search RAG pipeline, providing expert answers in just 1.4 seconds.
Accuracy: While all pipelines passed the LLM-Judge, GraphRAG achieved a near-perfect 0.9687 BERTScore, proving its semantic alignment with expert research context.
