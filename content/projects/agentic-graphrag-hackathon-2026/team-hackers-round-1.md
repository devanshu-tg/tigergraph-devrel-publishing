## Details

- **LLM:** Gemini
- **Vector database:** Chroma DB
- **Graph backend:** TigerGraph Community Edition (Note: The codebase uses pyTigerGraph RESTPP/GS endpoints, making it fully compatible with both TigerGraph Community Edition (Docker) and TigerGraph Savanna Cloud).
- **Average agent steps per question:** 4 steps
- **Retrieval methods:** Entity linking, graph traversal, similarity search, document retrieval, aggregation, temporal precedence traversal, conflict & contradiction resolution
