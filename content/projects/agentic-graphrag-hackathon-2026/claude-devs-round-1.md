TigerInvestigator  : Autonomous Agentic GraphRAG system that dynamically investigates complex questions and benchmarks RAG, GraphRAG, and Agentic GraphRAG using TigerGraph and evidence-driven retrieval.

## Details

- **LLM:** Claude Sonnet 5
- **Vector database:** FAISS (faiss-cpu)
- **Graph backend:** TigerGraph Savanna
- **Average agent steps per question:** 4
- **Retrieval methods:** Entity linking, similarity search, graph traversal, document retrieval, evidence evaluation
