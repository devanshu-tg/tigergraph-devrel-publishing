OlympiForge AI - An agentic GraphRAG system that autonomously investigates complex Olympic data by combining semantic search, knowledge-graph reasoning, multi-hop traversal, and evidence verification to deliver accurate, context-aware answers.

## Details

- **LLM:** Llama 3.2 (Ollama)
- **Vector database:** FAISS
- **Graph backend:** Embedded Graph Engine
- **Average agent steps per question:** Up to 5 steps
- **Retrieval methods:** Similarity search, entity linking, graph traversal, document retrieval, evidence evaluation, aggregation
