## How the team used TigerGraph

TigerGraph is used as the core intelligence layer where all detected security events are stored as a connected graph instead of isolated logs. We model relationships between users, attackers, websites, APIs, files, and attack events, allowing us to identify attack paths, detect coordinated threats, and calculate risk scores. This enables real-time correlation of phishing, API, and data leak incidents and provides a unified threat view in our dashboard.
